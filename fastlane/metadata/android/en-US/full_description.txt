Ritual is a private, mindful food photo journal for Android. It helps you remember meals, reflect on feelings and body cues, and notice journal patterns without calorie counting, food scores, or judgment.

Everything stays on your device. Ritual has no account, cloud journal, advertising, analytics SDK, subscription, or feature paywall.

Features:

* Capture breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks as private meal photos.
* Add feelings, reflections, hunger, craving, fullness, and an optional broad place label.
* Revisit daily journals, a gallery, a calendar, streaks, and journal-pattern insights.
* Schedule local meal reminders that skip meals already photographed.
* Lock the app with device authentication or a separate Ritual PIN.
* Export complete ZIP backups or date-filtered PDF and CSV reports.
* Create privacy-conscious share cards that omit notes, feelings, dates, and places.
* Choose light, dark, or system appearance and a personal intention.

Journal entries and captured photos remain in Android app-private storage. Approximate location is requested only when you ask for it, and Ritual stores a city-and-country label instead of newly obtained coordinates. Exports leave private app storage only when you explicitly create or share them.
