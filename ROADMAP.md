# Ritual Public Roadmap

This roadmap shows direction, not guaranteed dates. Priorities may change with security findings, user feedback, contributor capacity, and app-store requirements.

## Principles that will not change

- Free forever, with optional support that unlocks nothing
- Private and local-first by default
- No ads, behavioral analytics, or required account
- No calorie counting, diet scoring, or judgmental food labels
- Accessible, calm, and useful without turning reflection into a competition

## Now — stable Android release

- Stabilize the Android 1.x experience and automated signed releases
- Preserve permanent versioned APKs alongside the rolling latest build
- Complete public website, privacy, security, and contributor documentation
- Improve accessibility and test behavior across Android devices
- Prepare accurate Google Play testing, health-app, and data-safety declarations

## Next — broader public availability

- Publish a production Google Play release when testing requirements are met
- Improve import/export validation and recovery guidance
- Make documentation, translations, and small contributions easier
- Prioritize well-supported community requests that fit Ritual's principles

## Later — exploration

- Evaluate an iOS version with equivalent private storage, camera, authentication, reminders, and export behavior
- Evaluate additional trusted distribution channels such as F-Droid
- Explore new reflection and accessibility features without adding surveillance or judgment

## Not planned

- Advertisements or paid feature tiers
- Selling or profiling journal activity
- Required accounts or cloud storage by default
- Medical diagnosis, calorie targets, weight-loss scoring, or food morality labels

Issues marked [`good first issue`](https://github.com/ScoutorcusA/ritual-app/labels/good%20first%20issue) are intended to be approachable starting points. An issue label indicates scope, not a promise that a specific release will include the work.
