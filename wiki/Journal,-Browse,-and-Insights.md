# Journal, Browse, and Insights

## Journal

Entries are grouped by local calendar day. Each large day card contains:

- the full date;
- a visually separate moment count;
- a collage of up to four entries;
- a short feelings summary.

Tapping a day opens a dedicated daily journal with every entry from that day. Above the day cards, the summary shows total moments, entries per logged day, and the feeling selected on the greatest number of entries. When no feelings have been recorded, common feeling displays a dash. Ties are resolved alphabetically so the result remains stable.

Insight cards are inserted between daily cards in deterministic order. They describe correlations only; they do not diagnose health conditions or claim causation.

## Browse

Browse has Gallery and Calendar views.

- **Gallery:** filter by All, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack, and optionally by feeling.
- **Calendar:** a literal Sunday-to-Saturday monthly grid that scrolls vertically through months. A day with entries uses a meal photo in its cell; empty dates remain neutral. Tapping a date expands its entries below the month.

When a day has multiple entries, Ritual randomly chooses a highlight once and saves the entry ID in `daily_highlights`. The result remains stable across rebuilds and app launches. If that entry is deleted, Ritual removes the stale selection and chooses a valid replacement.

## Every insight rule

Rules operate on entries sorted newest first. At most one card is generated by each rule, in the order below.

### 1. Recent meal-type run

- **Window:** the uninterrupted run beginning with the newest entry.
- **Threshold:** 4 or more entries.
- **Match:** every entry in that run has the same meal type.
- **Example:** “Your last 6 entries have all been breakfast.”
- **Tie behavior:** none; only the newest run is evaluated.

### 2. Meal + feeling pattern

- **Window:** today plus the previous 6 calendar days.
- **Threshold:** the same meal type and feeling on at least 3 distinct days.
- **Counting:** multiple matching entries on one day count as one day.
- **Selection:** highest distinct-day count wins; equal counts sort by the internal meal/feeling key for stability.
- **Example:** “Breakfast felt happy on 5 days this past week.”

### 3. Meal + place pattern

- **Window:** today plus the previous 29 calendar days.
- **Threshold:** the same meal type and non-empty saved place label on at least 3 distinct days.
- **Counting:** repeated entries at that place on one day count as one day. Labels are matched exactly after trimming surrounding whitespace; coordinates are not required.
- **Selection:** highest distinct-day count wins; equal counts use stable key ordering.
- **Example:** “You had lunch in Columbus on 7 days this past month.”

### 4. Weekly journaling consistency

- **Window:** today plus the previous 6 calendar days.
- **Threshold:** at least 4 distinct days containing any entry.
- **Counting:** any number of meals on a day counts once.
- **Example:** “You paused to notice a meal on 6 days this week.”

If no rule reaches its threshold, no insight card is shown. Imported entries can contribute to later insights because insights are computed from the current journal, but importing does not show a streak celebration.
