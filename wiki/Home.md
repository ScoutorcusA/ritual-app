# Ritual Wiki

Ritual is an Android-first, local-only mindful food photo journal. It is designed for reflection rather than calorie counting: capture a meal, notice how it felt, and revisit patterns without judgment.

## Product principles

1. **Notice, do not score.** Ritual has no calories, targets, red/green foods, or nutrition judgments.
2. **Private by default.** There is no login, server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or automatic upload.
3. **Optional structure.** Reflection scales, reminders, streaks, locations, and notes can be omitted or disabled.
4. **Portable by choice.** Data leaves the Android app sandbox only when the user explicitly exports it.
